# Margwise scoring engine: written specification

Status: derived by reading the shipped bundle (`margwise-engine.js`, the only surviving copy; the original unminified source is not available). The specification and the independent reference implementation (`reference.py`) exist so that the engine can be reviewed, tested and replaced without trusting the bundle. `tests/golden.json` holds vectors generated by the bundle. `python3 engine/reference.py` and `node --test engine/tests` check agreement. Parameters are `uncalibrated`: they are design defaults, not fitted to data.

## Model

Six traits, in this order: R, I, A, S, E, C. A **scene** has four options; each option has loads on traits (its own trait 1.0, one adjacent trait 0.5). The player has **2 flasks** per scene and may put 0, 1 or 2 on an option (at most 2 in total, at most 2 per option).

For a trait vector theta (6 numbers), option utility is `u_o = beta * (loads_o . theta)` with beta = 1.5, plus a fixed "keep the oil" alternative with utility `keep` = 0.5. Choice probabilities are a softmax over the 5 utilities, `p`. A flask is a draw from `p`; the two flasks of a scene are treated as 2 independent draws, so the data for a scene is the vector of flask counts `n` over the four options (the keep alternative is the remainder).

## Estimate

MAP estimate with a Gaussian prior `theta ~ N(0, priorSd^2 I)`, `priorSd` = 1, by Newton iteration (at most 50 steps, stop when the largest step is below 1e-9) from theta = 0.

- Gradient: `g_t = -theta_t / priorSd^2 + sum_scenes sum_options beta * (n_o - 2 p_o) * load_{o,t}`
- Information: `H = I / priorSd^2 + sum_scenes 2 * beta^2 * (E_p[l l^T] - E_p[l] E_p[l]^T)` where `l` is the load vector and the expectation is over the four options only (the keep alternative has zero loads and is excluded from the sums but not from the softmax).
- Step: solve `H d = g`, `theta += d`. Covariance of the estimate: `H^-1`.

An echo scene (a repeat of an earlier scene) enters as another observation for the original scene.

## Result (`journeyResult`)

- `profile` = theta minus the mean of theta.
- `scores` = clamp(20 + 8 * profile, 0, 40).
- `code`: traits sorted by score descending, ties broken by trait order; the first three. `alternatives`: every ordering of traits within `tieWithin` = 3 score points of the third place, such that all chosen traits are within 3 of each other and every other trait is within 3 of all chosen ones (sorted by score then trait order).
- `confidence`: `broad` if max(theta) - min(theta) < 1.4; else `clear` if minZ >= 0.7; else `close`. `minZ` is the smallest, over each of the top three traits i (by theta) and each other trait j, of `(theta_i - theta_j) / sqrt(max(H^-1_ii + H^-1_jj - 2 H^-1_ij, 1e-12))`.
- `lowEngagement`: at least half (`noneShare` 0.5) of all recorded scenes had zero flasks spent.
- `stopsPlayed`: number of non-echo scenes.
- `consistency`: if an echo scene exists and its original exists, `overlap` = (sum over options of min(flasks in original, flasks in echo)) / 2; `mixed` = overlap < 0.5.

## Adaptive walk (`step`)

Given the choices so far:
1. If every scene has been played: done.
2. If at least `echoAfter` = 6 non-echo scenes have been played and no echo has happened, take the played scene with the most flasks on a single option (the first such scene in play order wins ties); if that maximum is above 0, the next scene is an echo of it.
3. Otherwise compute the estimate. Stop when at least `minStops` = 8 scenes were played and minZ >= `stopZ` = 1.
4. Else the next scene is the unplayed scene maximising `trace(Cov . Info_scene(theta))`, first in bank order winning ties (strictly greater than the current best by more than 1e-12).

## Option display order (`displayOptions(scene, seed)`)

`h = seed >>> 0`; for each character code `c` of the scene id: `h = (imul(h xor c, 16777619) + 2654435769) >>> 0`. A generator seeded with `h`: `t = (t + 1831565813) >>> 0; z = t; z = imul(z xor (z >>> 15), z | 1); z ^= z + imul(z xor (z >>> 7), z | 61); return ((z xor (z >>> 14)) >>> 0) / 2^32`. Fisher-Yates from the end: for i = n-1 down to 1, `j = floor(rand() * (i + 1))`, swap `i` and `j`.

## Career fit (`careerPaths`)

Each profile vector (the player's `profile`, and a career's six O*NET interest values) is z-scored across the six traits with the population standard deviation (a flat vector has no fit). The fit `r` is the mean product of the two z-score vectors, clamped to [-1, 1]. Careers are sorted by `r` descending, then by career id.

## Not specified here

Field ranking (mean of the four best careers per field, converted to a percentile against the reference table in `data/content.json`), the "cautious" and "no signal" display rules, and the puzzle layer live in the page, not in the engine. They are described in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and checked by `engine/tests/field-ranking.test.mjs`.

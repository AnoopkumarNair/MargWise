# Golden cases, matcher 1.2.0 (slice S2)

**For the owner to read (BUILD_PLAN T2.4, ASK).** There are 15 cases across 5 occupation families. Each row is a made-up person run against a test slice. Thirteen cases use hand-built test slices, not real data: the weights, AI shares, pay numbers, city shares and the credential catalogue are invented to exercise the rules. Two use real-shape slices, extracts of the O*NET 31.0 slices frozen in `fixtures/`: csr-real-near (Customer Service Representatives) and acc-real-tools (Accountants and Auditors). Please read the roads, the kinds and the flags, and say whether each one makes sense. The questions at the end are the places where SPEC 7.2 leaves a gap.

Matcher 1.2.0 keeps every rule of 1.1.0 (owner decisions O1 to O4) and adds SPEC 7.2 to 7.4: the target's level and the experience fit, the road kind, the pay change, the three flags where their data exists, and bridges. Coverage, reach and the order of the 11 earlier cases did not change.

How to read it:

- **Level** is the person's level from 1 to 6 (SPEC 7.2): total years, plus 1 for leading 6 or more people or owning a budget.
- **Roads** are in the order the app would show them, with the reach and the coverage (the share of the target's skill needs the person already has). Adjacent is 0.70 or more, stretch 0.40 to 0.70, leap under 0.40.
- **Kind** (SPEC 7.2): *up* (same O*NET minor group, a higher level), *across* (same level, or lower but not a reset), *diagonal* (another minor group, a higher level), *reset* (the job starts 2 or more levels below the person, and they cover under half of it), *fork* (into management, or a known specialist step in the same group). "Kind not known" means the role has no level yet (a new role without a level in its catalogue entry).
- **Level n (fit)** is the target's typical entry level, from its job zone (or from a new role's own levels), and whether the person's years are below, in or above that level's year band.
- **Flags** never hide a road. *below_pay_floor*: the pay change is under the lowest change the person accepts. *needs_relocation*: under half the openings are in places the person accepts. *outside_time*: the suggested bridges need more than 12 months at the person's study hours. Most cases have no flags, because their slices carry no pay or city data and no credential catalogue is passed.
- **Bridges** are credentials from a reviewed catalogue that cover the most gap weight (up to 3). Here the catalogue is made up for tests (`demo-` ids). Without a catalogue the road says "No reviewed course yet" and lists the skills.
- **Order** uses the default sliders (short move 3; pay, AI safety and demand 1) unless the case says otherwise. Pay is measured only in bfsi-pay-floor.

What each case shows:

- **acc-core:** with Used at 0.85, Credit Analysts is a close move. Treasurers and Controllers is a **fork** (into management). Data Scientists is a **diagonal** step: another minor group, one level up.
- **acc-rusty:** skills last used more than 3 years ago count as rusty (0.4), so every road drops a band compared with acc-core. This person is level 5 (8 years and leads 6-20), and Budget, Credit and Financial Analysts start at level 3 with coverage just under 0.5, so the rule calls them **reset** ("start again lower"). See question K2.
- **swdev-data:** Database Administrators and Systems Analysts are **up** (same minor group 15-12, level 3 above the person's 2). The LLM Evaluation Specialist on this older fixture has no levels, so its kind is not known.
- **swdev-qa-near:** near() credits LLM evaluation at 0.46 through quality control analysis, which lifts that road from a leap to a stretch.
- **csr-creds:** ITIL 4 is valid, so it credits ServiceNow, computers and problem solving at 0.85. COPC expired in 2023, so its skills count 0.4. Office Clerks starts at level 1, two below this level-3 person, with coverage 0.21: a reset.
- **nurse-core:** Nurse Practitioners is a **fork** because it is a known specialist step for a registered nurse (`matcher/ladder.json`, a draft for you to review). Medical and Health Services Managers is a fork into management. Health Information Technologists starts two levels down with coverage 0.43: a reset.
- **nurse-lead-short:** only the short-move slider is on. This senior nurse is level 6, so Nurse Practitioners (levels 4-5) with coverage 0.44 is a **reset** by the rule, not a fork: the reset rule is checked first. See question K2.
- **csr-real-near** and **acc-real-tools:** unchanged from 1.1.0, now with kinds from the real job zones.
- **near-rank:** tests the near rule (O2); unchanged.
- **qa-llm-adjacent (new):** a road into a **new AI-era role** that is a close move (TEST_PLAN layer 2). LLM Evaluation Specialist takes its levels (2-4) from the role itself, so it is across for a level-3 person. The other kinds are all here: up (Research Scientists, same group 15-12), diagonal (Data Scientists, 15-20), fork (Quality Control Systems Managers). Python is rated rusty, so the Python course is the bridge on most roads.
- **bpm-lead-reset (new):** a **reset into a new AI-era role** (TEST_PLAN layer 2): AI Trainer starts at level 1, five below this level-6 team lead, who covers 0.20 of it. The **expired COPC** credits monitoring at 0.4 only, so it comes back as a bridge marked "renew?". The AI Trainer bridges need 160 hours, more than a year at 5 hours a month: outside_time. Customer Service is a step down with high coverage: SPEC 7.2 has no kind for it, so it is "across" (question K1).
- **bfsi-pay-floor (new, 5th family):** **every road falls below the pay floor.** The slice carries made-up pay numbers; every target pays 5 to 50 percent less and the person accepts no cut, so every road has below_pay_floor and none is hidden. Pay is measured, so it now counts in the order. Personal Financial Advisors openings are mostly in Mumbai, Delhi NCR and Bengaluru, and this person is only open to Pune: needs_relocation. Insurance Sales has 45 percent in Pune plus 10 percent remote: no flag.
- **csr-cert-bridge (new):** **a certification closes a gap** (T1.8): the valid ServiceNow certificate credits ServiceNow and computers at 0.85, which turns Computer User Support into a close move (0.91). A credential the person holds and that is valid is never offered as a bridge. Training and Development needs 150 hours of courses: outside_time at 5 hours a month.

Questions for the owner (SPEC 7.2 leaves these open; the matcher uses the choice shown until you decide):

- **K1. Moves the kind table does not name.** (a) Same minor group at the same or a lower level: *across*. (b) Another minor group two or more levels up: *diagonal*. (c) A lower level that is not a reset (coverage 0.5 or more): *across*, as for Customer Service in bpm-lead-reset. Options: keep these; add a "step down" kind; or show no kind.
- **K2. Reset for senior people.** The reset rule compares the person's level with the target's *entry* level. A level-5 or level-6 person therefore gets "reset" on many specialist roads that start at level 3 or 4 whenever coverage is under 0.5 (acc-rusty, nurse-lead-short). Options: keep it; compare with the target's top level instead; or require both conditions plus "below the target's band".
- **K3. Fork into management.** SPEC says "same minor group, and the target is a management occupation (SOC 11-)". Every manager is in SOC 11-, so taken word for word no one who is not already a manager could ever have a management fork. The matcher reads it as: any SOC 11- target is a fork for a person who is not a manager; a manager moving to another manager role follows the level rules. Specialist steps need the same minor group and an entry in `matcher/ladder.json`.
- **K4. The specialist step table** (`matcher/ladder.json`) is a draft with 4 steps, each an O*NET related occupation in the same minor group at a higher job zone. Approve, edit or empty it.
- **K5. outside_time.** SPEC does not say how much study time is too much. The matcher raises it when the suggested bridges need more than 12 months at the top of the person's hours band ('40+' never). Approve or change the 12 months.

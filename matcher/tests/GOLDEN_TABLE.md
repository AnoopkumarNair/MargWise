# Golden cases, matcher 1.1.0 (slice S1)

For the owner to read (BUILD_PLAN T2.4). Each row is a made-up person run against a test slice. Nine slices are hand-built test fixtures, not real data: the weights and AI shares are invented to exercise the rules. Two use real-shape slices, extracts of the O*NET 31.0 slices frozen in `fixtures/`: csr-real-near (Customer Service Representatives) and acc-real-tools (Accountants and Auditors).

Matcher 1.1.0 applies the owner decisions of 2026-10-29 (deviations, "Decisions" O1 to O4): Used counts 0.85, near counts only from the 3 closest neighbours at 0.90 or more, tool weights in exported slices are capped at the median of the other needs, and the default sliders are short move 3, pay 1, AI safety 1, demand 1.

How to read it:

- **Level** is the person's level from 1 to 6 (SPEC 7.2): total years, plus 1 for leading 6 or more people or owning a budget.
- **Roads** are in the order the app would show them, with the reach and the coverage (the share of the target's skill needs the person already has).
- **Reach:** adjacent is 0.70 or more, stretch is 0.40 to 0.70, leap is under 0.40. The app hides leaps behind "show leaps".
- **Order** uses the default sliders (short move 3; pay, AI safety and demand 1) unless the case says otherwise. Pay and demand are "not yet measured", so they are equal for every road and do not change the order. In S1 the order comes from coverage and AI safety (1 minus the observed AI use), with coverage counting three times as much.
- **Flags** are empty in every case. The S1 slices carry no city, pay or study-hours data, so no flag can be raised yet.

What each case shows:

- **acc-core:** with Used at 0.85, Credit Analysts moves from a stretch (0.68) to a close move (0.73). With short move at 3, Bookkeeping (close, 0.77) comes third; under the old equal sliders it was fifth, behind a leap, because AI already does much of that work.
- **acc-rusty:** skills last used more than 3 years ago count as rusty (0.4), so every road drops a band compared with acc-core. Excel is rated strong, so it keeps full credit.
- **swdev-qa-near:** the person has no LLM evaluation skill, but has the nearby quality control skill, its closest neighbour at 0.92. near() credits it at 0.46, which lifts the LLM Evaluation Specialist road from a leap (0.37) to a stretch (0.49). The Selenium pair (0.6) is under the 0.90 floor and no longer counts.
- **csr-creds:** ITIL 4 is valid, so it credits ServiceNow, computers and problem solving at the used level (0.85). COPC expired in 2023, so its skills count 0.4. The person rated coordination strong, and their own rating wins.
- **nurse-lead-short:** only the short-move slider is on, so the roads are in coverage order. The enjoy slider is ignored, because the interest walk was not taken.
- **csr-real-near:** no near data is passed in; the matcher reads the slice's own pairs (slice amendment A1). The export now keeps only the pairs that can count (86 in this extract). Without near, coverage is 0.20 to 0.27; with it, 0.33 to 0.41, and Credit Authorizers becomes a stretch.
- **near-rank (new in 1.1.0):** tests the near rule (O2) on hand-built near data. Systems analysis has five neighbours; Java is the third closest (0.95; it ties with skl:2.C.4.a, which sorts first by id), so Java credits it. SQL is fourth (0.94), so it does not, even though the person rates SQL strong. Computers and electronics has one neighbour at 0.89, under the floor: no credit. Quality control has one at exactly 0.90: credit.
- **acc-real-tools (new in 1.1.0):** shows the tool cap (O3). The person ticks all 10 tools of the real accountant slice as used, plus 5 core skills. Every tool in each list weighs at most the median of the other needs (0.42 in the accountant's own list, against 0.8 or 0.6 in the graph). With the graph weights the five roads would cover 0.31 to 0.38 and Bookkeeping would be second; with the cap they cover 0.29 to 0.35 and Tax Preparers is second. Every road is still a leap: tools alone do not make a close move.

No questions are open on this table. The question about road order (AI safety counting as much as coverage) was answered by the owner on 2026-10-29 (O4): short move now counts 3 by default.

Margwise content review

The rewritten story, the 60 option lines, and the career and school-path content for twelve fields. Please mark up anything that is wrong, unclear or unsuitable for Class 9 to 12 students.

What to check, in order

  1. Story: do the three chapters, the recurring friend Ila and the collected note read well for a 14 to 17 year old?
  2. Option lines: does each new line still say the same thing as the old one? Ids and scoring are unchanged, so a line must keep the same type of activity (the letter shown next to it).
  3. Fields and careers: are the right careers in the right fields? Are any missing for India?
  4. School paths: every exam and eligibility line needs checking against the official site. Lines marked "Check before relying on this" are the ones where sources disagreed.

1. The story

The road, the lamplighter who has gone, Jyo the firefly and the lighthouse stay. New: three chapters, a recurring friend, a collectible note, and a named keeper in each town.

Three chapters

  • Chapter I: The Road Wakes (starts after 0 lamps lit). The first lamps are the hardest. Light a few, and the road starts to remember itself.
  • Chapter II: Hills and Rivers (starts after 4 lamps lit). The road climbs and bends. Somewhere ahead, a girl with a ferry lantern is looking for the lighthouse too.
  • Chapter III: The Salt Wind (starts after 8 lamps lit). You can smell the sea. The lighthouse is close, and so is the last piece of the note.

Places come in an adaptive order, so chapters follow the number of lamps lit, not a fixed list of towns.

Ila, who keeps turning up

  • After lamp 3: Ahead on the road, a girl with a ferry lantern waves. "I am Ila. I saw your lamp from the water."
  • After lamp 6: Ila catches up, carrying a flask of lamp oil. "The lighthouse needs this. Walk with me a while."
  • After lamp 9: Ila points past the hills to the dark sea. "The lighthouse. Look how dark it still is."
  • After lamp 12: Ila hands you her lantern. "Almost there. Whatever you carry, it is enough."

The lamplighter's note

Each lit lamp adds a scrap of a note the old lamplighter left. After eight lamps it is whole.

A lamp burns best for hands that like the work. Light the road for the ones who walk behind you.

2. The 15 places and 60 options

The letter is the type of activity the option measures: R Builder, I Thinker, A Creator, S Helper, E Persuader, C Organiser. The old options often joined two activities in one line ("study X, then sketch Y"). The new ones name one.

Usual stream

Science with Biology (PCB) is the usual choice. Chemistry and Physics matter too.

Class 11 and 12 subjects

  • CBSE: English, Physics, Chemistry, Biology.
  • ICSE/ISC: English, Physics, Chemistry, Biology.
  • State/other: Science stream with Biology (PCB).

Entrance exams

NEET-UG (run by NTA) is the entrance test for MBBS, BDS, BAMS, BHMS, BUMS and BVSc. Nursing, pharmacy and physiotherapy routes differ by state and institution, so check the current rules for each.

Degree

MBBS takes 5.5 years including internship. BDS takes 5 years. B.Pharm and B.Sc Nursing take 4 years. Specialising means a postgraduate degree after that.

Try it early

Shadow a doctor, volunteer at a clinic, or take a first-aid course.

Good to know

Government medical seats are extremely competitive. Nursing, pharmacy, physiotherapy and other allied health courses are strong paths too.

Check before relying on this

  • Nursing and physiotherapy admission routes vary and have been changing. Confirm with the state authority and the council.

Where to check: NEET-UG (NTA), National Medical Commission, O*NET OnLine (interest profiles)

4. Decisions and next steps

Decisions for you

  • Question formats. Today every place is "spend two flasks across four options". I can make places feel different without changing what is measured, by changing the interaction (drag a flask, stock a stall, pick first and second). A truly different format, such as a slider, changes the measurement and needs a psychometric check.
  • Number of places. The engine asks for 8 to 15. About 10 would feel tighter, but this setting lives in the scoring engine and should be changed with the instrument owner.
  • Who reviews what. Question wording, school-path facts and the career list each need a named reviewer.

Proposed next steps

  1. You review this pack. I apply the changes.
  2. Fact check. A counsellor or subject expert checks every school-path line against official sites. Plan to repeat this every admission cycle, because exam rules change each year.
  3. Instrument check. The scoring is still marked uncalibrated. Pilot with 30 to 50 students, compare with an established interest inventory such as the O*NET Interest Profiler, and test whether results repeat a few weeks later.
  4. Art. When the image quota resets or a paid plan is added: four test images, then lock a style, then Jyo in five or six poses, layered backdrops and one consistent icon set for the options.
  5. Question variety. Build one or two new interaction types over the same scoring, and playtest them.
  6. Playtest. Five to eight students on a mid-range Android phone, watching for confusion and boredom, not asking.
  7. Real app. Accounts with parent consent for under-18s (India's DPDP Act), a backend (Supabase or Cloudflare), asset hosting on a CDN, a parent view, and Hindi and other languages.

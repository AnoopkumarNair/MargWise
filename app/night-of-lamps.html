What kind of work would suit you?

Margwise is a story game for students in Class 9 to 12. You walk a road at night, light lamps and make small choices. At the end you see which interests stand out and which careers are worth a closer look.

About 15 minutes. No marks and no wrong answers. Best with a parent beside you.

You are one of the first people to try this. Your feedback helps us fix it before other students see it.

What we keep, and who sees it

We keep your name, the choices you make in the game and how long each place takes, your puzzle answers, the class, board and stream you pick, your results, your screen size and any feedback you write. We never ask for your phone number, email or address. A scrambled version of your connection address is kept for 24 hours only, to stop abuse.

The Margwise team can see it. If you joined through a school, the counsellor at that school can also see your name, whether you finished, and your results. Nobody else can. We use it to improve the game and to check how well it measures interests.

We keep it until the test ends, about 31 January 2027, then delete it. If your parent also agreed to follow-up checks, the school counsellor keeps your first name, class and their later notes for up to 12 months after the test, and nothing else. To have yours deleted sooner, tell the person who invited you.

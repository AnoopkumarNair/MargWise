# ADR 008: AI exposure as two published lenses, never one score

- **Status:** Proposed — awaiting Anu's approval
- **Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Spec:** 04 §12

## Context

AI exposure evidence is young and methodologically diverse (05 §7).

## Decision

Show ILO 2025 gradient (Lens A) and Anthropic Economic Index usage (Lens B) side by side with dates, caveats and method. The contract has no combined-score field (a test rejects one). The money engine's δ comes from a published, labelled-assumption mapping.

## Consequences

Honest but less 'simple' for families; mitigated by plain-language copy.

## Alternatives considered

Single blended score (false precision).

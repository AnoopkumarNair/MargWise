## 4. What kept her going / what nearly lost her

Top 3 kept her going:
1. The mystery lines: "She left three nights ago and took no oil. Will you help me?" (Ila, Clockmaker's Lane) and "The road was never mine. Choose yours." (The lamplighter, Herbalists' Garden).
2. The "it noticed me" lines: "You like helping people." (Salt Harbour), "Slow, careful, curious." (Observatory Hill), "Puzzles light you up. Jyo noticed." (Printers' Quarter).
3. The result screen: the radar and "Fits your Helper, Thinker" tags on the field cards tied the game to school science, and the "Science with Biology (PCB) is the usual choice" line matched her real class.

Top 3 that nearly lost her:
1. The length. The cover says "About 10 to 15 minutes"; she spent about 30. Sharpen says "up to 7 more towns, about 1 to 2 minutes each", which is true, but she felt the pull to stop at Festival Square (interest 5): "Coach the junior team, meh."
2. Three screens between the last town and the result: "Two quick things first" (class, board, mind), "Lighthouse is lit" and "See your shape". Each one is a small step, but together they felt like more homework.
3. Small text in the draft and result screens. The draft screen middle is empty. On the result screen "Less strong for now", "How sure is this?" and "AI EXPOSURE" are small, and she had to lean in. "Veterinarian" is only found after "More: all careers, AI exposure and pay" and then scrolling past 8 careers. She nearly quit the careers search before she found it.

## 5. Did it notice her? Did the mystery land?
Yes, mostly. The "You like helping people", "Slow, careful, curious" and "Puzzles light you up" lines felt like the game was watching her choices, and she liked them. The mystery landed at the lamplighter's letter (interest 8): "I put this light out myself... The road was never mine." The Ila and Jyo lines kept the thread going. The weak point: Festival Square and Glass Cliffs felt like filler, so the mystery lost some pull in the middle of the sharpen phase.

## 6. Does the result fit? Would she share it?
Partly. Thinker, Helper, Organiser fits her: she likes science and helping people. Health and medicine is "Start here" and Animal Scientist is close to vet, so the result points the right way. It does not name veterinary science or music therapy on the first screens. Music therapy is not in the data at all (only "Musician or Singer" under Performing arts). She would share the radar with her mother and a friend, but she would not send it yet, because it does not say "veterinary" in plain words. She would share the "Veterinarian" line once she found it.

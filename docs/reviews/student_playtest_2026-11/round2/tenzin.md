## 4. What kept her going / what nearly lost her

Kept her going:
1. Choices that matched her real interests and sounded like school: "Test apple mixes to find why one tastes best" and "Test the water to find what makes people ill". She picked the testing one each time.
2. Hooks that promised a payoff: "She stayed one night only. Then she went on." and Jyo: "I know where she went. I promised not to say."
3. The result gave one clear next step: "Start here" on Science and research, with "Thinker ●●●" and the school path "Class 11–12 ... Science, with Maths or Biology depending on your subject."

Nearly lost her:
1. Parent consent: "Has a parent or guardian agreed? ... Students under 18 should not do it without that." She clicked "Not yet. I will ask first." The screen went back to the sign-up with no message, and the box was still ticked. She had no way to continue without lying, so she said "A parent has agreed". This is the worst moment of the game for an under-18 student in a school lab.
2. Sign-up small print: "Data: what we keep and who sees it" runs about 320 words above the Begin button. She skipped most of it. "The counsellor at that school can also see your name, whether you finished, and your results." She did not like that.
3. Science field page: "Real AI use seen so far (Anthropic, 2025 usage) covers about 27% of this job." and "1,875 words" on one scroll. "What is AI exposure?" She stopped reading.

## 5. Did the story notice her? Did the mystery land?

Partly. The choices in the story were fair to her: testing, careful work, climbing, a quiet river camp. The result matched her biology interest, and she said "This is me" at the Thinker card.

But the lines reacting to her choices were generic in places. "You look closely before you act" appeared twice (Herbalists' Garden and Healing House). "Jyo hums softly. People matter to you" after she picked coaching felt like a guess, not a noticing. Noticing score for her: 6 of 10.

The mystery: the lamplighter's letter ("I put this light out myself... The road was never mine. Choose yours.") and Ila ("She was never lost. She went ahead to make room for you.") make the lamplighter sound like a teacher who wants students to find their own road. That is a warm message, but it does not answer "where did she go". Jyo's "I promised not to say" is never followed up, and the finale ("Every lamp you lit caught. This is the light you carry.") does not pay it off. She said: "So where did she go? Okay, whatever." Mystery ending did not land for her. The emotional close was weaker than the set-up.

## 6. Does the result fit? Would she share it?

Fit: mostly yes. Thinker and Science fit her biology, and the school path ("Science, with Maths or Biology") gave her something to do. Builder fits her football and raft-building choices. Creator ("imagining new things") is weaker, but she did choose the mountain video, so it is fair.

Mismatch she noticed: her football and trekking are not on the result. "Sports and fitness" appears only as a small "Any stream" card at the bottom of the field list. She did not open it.

Would she share? Not with classmates. She might show the teacher the login code and the Science card. She said: "I will tell Sir the Science part. The rest is for me." Would not post it.

## 4. What kept him going / what nearly lost him

Kept him going (briefly):
1. The cover picture and the words "Begin" / "About 8 to 10 minutes" (shot_000). Short, clear button.
2. The form is short when he looks only at the big labels: "Your name", "Invite code", and the one checkbox.

Nearly lost him (quoted text):

## 7. Bugs and layout problems
- **Consent loop, no way forward (serious, shot_005 and shot_006):** "Not yet. I will ask first." closes the popup and returns to the form with no message. There is no "ask a parent and come back" screen, and no note that the game can be used later. A student who answers honestly is stuck with no guidance.
- **Double gate, same question:** the form checkbox "I am 18 or older, or my parent or guardian knows about this and agrees." (shot_004) is asked again in the popup. The checkbox can be ticked without reading, and then the popup asks the same thing. Repeated question, two different answers possible.
- **Checkbox state after "Not yet":** the checkbox stays ticked (shot_006), so the form looks ready to submit even though the student said no parent agreed.
- **Long English on the sign-up screen (shot_001):** the data paragraph (about 170 words) sits directly under the form. For a student with reading difficulty this is a wall of text. Consider a short summary with a "Read more" link.
- **Unclear words for this student (not translated, no help):** "Prototype" and "drafts" (cover: "Places and words are drafts"), "invite code", "connection address", "scrambled", "counsellor", "follow-up checks", "retained"/"delete", "stream" and "board" (in the data paragraph), "interest guide", "not a test of ability".
- **Tablet layout:** the cover (shot_000) and the form (shot_006) fit the 820 px width with no horizontal scroll. The modal on the form (shot_005) is centred and readable. No layout bugs seen on tablet.

4. **Top 3 that kept her going**
   - Night Bazaar: "Pitch your stall to buyers, even if some walk away." Her choices here were the most like her.
   - The lamplighter letter: "I walked ahead and tried every kind of work, so you could find yours. The road was never mine. Choose yours."
   - The field card: "Business and finance ... Fits your Persuader ... Start here." This was the first result that felt like her.

   **Top 3 that nearly lost her**
   - The sign-up wall: "Data: what we keep and who sees it" plus "Has a parent or guardian agreed?" Two consent questions in a row, and the checkbox had already said it.
   - Clockmaker's Lane and Herbalists' Garden: "Build a repair tracker that flags what is due ... though bugs may eat your evening." Too many towns where no option felt like her.
   - The draft: "More towns are unlikely to change this picture." Combined with about 18 minutes against an 8 to 10 minute promise, this made her feel time was wasted. She took the result to stop the clock.

5. **Story and noticing.** Mostly no. Reactions were generic: "Some of each. Jyo smiles." and "A little of both. That's fine." After she chose the pitch and price list, the lamp line was "A lit lamp is the best sign a stall can have." That does not respond to her pitching. Ila's lines ("Everyone says she was happy. Nobody says she was lost.") and the lamplighter letter did land a little. The mystery ending was not reached in her run: she never saw the lighthouse finale, only the letter.

6. **Result fit.** The field order is off for her: "Business and finance" is close to what she secretly wants, but "Helper" is ranked first and she does not see herself that way. Economics is not named anywhere. Accountancy appears only as a school subject under Helper, which is confusing. The "Business and finance" and "Law and justice" cards fit. The result says a lot about her, but it does not give her economics. Would she share? Not publicly. She might show her parents the Business and finance card, or screenshot it for herself. She would not send the whole result to a friend.

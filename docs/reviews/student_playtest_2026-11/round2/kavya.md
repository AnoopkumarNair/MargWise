## 4. What kept her going / what nearly lost her

Kept going:
1. Healing House: "Test the water to find what makes people ill, though most tests show nothing." Her interest went to 9.
2. The lamplighter letter: "I walked ahead and tried every kind of work, so you could find yours." It made her want the result.
3. Health and medicine, then "Veterinarian: Treats animals, from pets to farm animals." The first place she saw her own goal listed.

Nearly lost her:
1. The sign-up and data page: "Data: what we keep and who sees it" is three long paragraphs in small text, before the story starts.
2. The town choices: each one is 12 to 20 words in small text, and each town has four of them. Night Bazaar's "Repair a neighbour's broken phone at the stall, with tiny screws and bad light" was a no.
3. The result pages: "All 16 fields, by stream" and a 2,000-word field page. The labels "AI exposure: high" and "No India pay figure for this job yet" are jargon she skims.

## 5. Did the story notice her? Did the ending land?

- Partly. Warden Karan ("She tried my work for a day. She was trying them all.") and Jyo ("Your road is getting clearer") felt like they noticed what she was doing. Most town reactions are templated ("Some of each. Jyo smiles.", "You split your oil. Fair enough.") and feel generic after the fourth one.
- Ila's line "She would have liked this town" after Healing House felt personal.
- The mystery ending landed. "She was never lost. She went ahead to make room for you." and the lamplighter's "The road was never mine. Choose yours." gave her a reason to finish.

## 6. Does the result fit? Would she share it?

- Helper and Thinker fit her well: she agrees with both. Organiser is a partial fit (she organises shelter rotas, but she did not feel it as a strong part of herself).
- No signal for music or animals in the shape itself, even though she sings and volunteers. The interest shape is about the choices, so music shows up only as a field she has to look for ("Performing arts and music", with Musician or Singer).
- Vet is in the fields, but the school path explains NEET, MBBS and BAMS. It does not explain BVSc (veterinary) or the vet entrance route. She would ask her counsellor.
- Music therapy is not in the results at all.
- Would she share it? Not the shape with friends. She might show the "Veterinarian" card to her mum and the school counsellor.

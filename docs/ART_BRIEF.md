# Art brief for ChatGPT and Gemini

Shared style (paste at the start of every prompt):
"Soft painterly children's storybook illustration for teenagers. Warm lantern glow against a deep indigo dusk sky (#141a3a to #2b2f63) with amber and gold light (#ffcf6b, #f2a03d) and cream highlights (#fff3d6). Gentle, magical, hopeful. Clean shapes, no harsh outlines, no photorealism, no anime. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, signs or captions anywhere in the image."

Rules for both tools: ask for ONE image per request, no text in the picture (we typeset words ourselves), and keep the lantern flame as the recurring light source.
- ChatGPT can give a transparent PNG: say "transparent background, PNG".
- Gemini cannot: say "on a flat solid pure magenta (#FF00FF) background, nothing else in the frame", and we will remove it.

## 1. Logo mark (most important)
"A single small brass-and-glass hand lantern, front view, centred, with a bright teardrop flame inside and a soft glow around it. A tiny curved road of three lit lamp dots rises from the base of the lantern, leading up like a path. Flat vector style with a few soft gradients, bold simple shapes that stay readable at 32 pixels. Colours: cream #fff3d6, gold #ffcf6b, amber #f2a03d, deep indigo #141a3a. Square 1:1, 2048 px, lots of empty margin. No text." Ask for 4 variations: (a) full colour, (b) one-colour cream on indigo, (c) one-colour indigo on cream, (d) simplified for tiny sizes.

## 2. App icon
"The same lantern mark, centred, filling about 60 percent of a square, on a rounded deep-indigo night gradient with a faint lighthouse beam behind it. 1024 x 1024, no text, important content inside the centre 80 percent."

## 3. Jyo, the firefly guide (4 poses, one prompt each, same character)
Character: "A friendly firefly with a round glowing lantern-like abdomen, big kind eyes, tiny translucent wings, small antennae. Warm golden glow. Cute but not babyish."
Poses: (a) hovering, neutral, facing right; (b) flying fast diagonally upward, delighted; (c) drooping and dim, sleepy; (d) pointing a tiny arm towards the right, encouraging. "Full body, centred, 1024 x 1024, transparent background." Keep the same face and colours in all four. Tip: generate pose (a) first, then say "same character, now ..." in the same chat.

## 4. Missing place: Festival Square (background)
"Festival Square at dusk: a wide cobbled square where all the lamps of the road will gather, still bare, with empty stalls, strings of unlit lamps and a tall wooden welcome arch not yet decorated, hills and a deep-indigo sky behind. No people in the foreground. Vertical 2:3, 1024 x 1536, centred composition, calm space in the top third and bottom third for text."

## 5. Field icons (16, one prompt each, same style)
"A single round badge icon, 1024 x 1024, transparent background, soft painterly flat style, gold rim, one clear symbol in the centre, no text. Symbol: ..." Symbols: Health and medicine: stethoscope and heart; Science and research: flask and atom; Engineering: gear and blueprint compass; Technology and data: laptop with a glowing node network; Business and finance: coin stack and rising bars; Design and creative arts: pencil and colour palette; Understanding people: two profiles with a speech bubble; Law and justice: balanced scales; Media, writing and teaching: open book and quill; Public service and defence: pillared building with a flag; Travel, hospitality and aviation: aeroplane and bell; Sports and fitness: running shoe and medal; Agriculture and environment: sprouting plant and sun; Skilled trades: spanner and hard hat; Performing arts and music: music note and mask; Maritime and logistics: ship and anchor.

## Caption options
1. Light your way. (short; current placeholder)
2. Find the work that fits you. (plain and descriptive; recommended as the sub-line)
3. One lamp at a time.
Wordmark: we will typeset "Margwise" in Fraunces ourselves, so do not ask for text in the image.
